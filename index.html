THE DETAILS

A few things
worth knowing.

Is BidVello a government agency?

No. It is an independent proposed software product and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any government agency.

Does it submit bids automatically?

No. BidVello is intended to discover and organize opportunities. Your company remains responsible for eligibility, bid preparation and submission.

Are the opportunities in the demo real?

No. All preview records are fictional examples. Live official-source data must be connected and verified before launch.

Does a match mean we qualify?

No. A match label — Strong search match or Possible search match — means the notice satisfies your profile’s stated search criteria: NAICS and PSC codes, set-aside, geography and keywords. BidVello has not screened your capability, capacity, bonding or qualifications, and a label is never an eligibility determination. Read the complete solicitation before bidding.

Is BidVello the complete SAM.gov feed?

No. BidVello matches the public federal postings against your profile, and it is not a complete feed: opportunities subject to documented business conflicts are excluded, and BidVello says so here and in its terms rather than quietly. If completeness matters more than focus, SAM.gov’s own saved searches are free.

What if a deadline BidVello sends is wrong?

We investigate promptly, correct the record, and notify every subscriber who received the wrong information. If the error materially impaired the service and cannot reasonably be cured, affected subscribers receive a credit or refund for the affected period under the refund policy. An error the government source itself published is identified as a source correction.

Where do the opportunities come from?

Public federal postings on SAM.gov, checked on a recurring schedule with the retrieval and verification times shown on each alert. BidVello is an independent service and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by SAM.gov, the U.S. General Services Administration, or any agency.
